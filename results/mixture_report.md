> The reasoning share of 7.03% is fundable **only if at least 65.3%** of the nominal 85.0B survives quality gating. The one component where survival could be measured rather than assumed survived at **24.5%**. The other 78B is answer-verified rather than step-verified, so PRM800K's rate is a worst case and is not assumed lane-wide - but the gap is wide enough that the share cannot be called settled. Gate: Sample 2,000 traces from each of OpenThoughts-3 and Nemotron and measure survival under the same verified-answer-plus-explicit-check gate, before stage 1 of the proxy. If pooled survival lands below 65.3%, the reasoning share drops to whatever 4 epochs of measured supply funds and the shortfall is bought as distillation rather than assumed - the identical rule the agentic lane already runs under.
