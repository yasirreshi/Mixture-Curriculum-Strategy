| reasoning | 18B | 0.0% | **unreachable at this fill rate** |
| stem_math | 14B | 0.0% | **unreachable at this fill rate** |

> This is the whole argument for the reserve in one table. The lanes fed by curated sources fill easily - a public-domain book *is* long-context reserve material, so that fill rate is high by construction. The two lanes the model is actually being built for do not fill: at the measured 0.6% agentic fill rate, a 10B agentic reserve would need ~1.7T of cleaned agentic-lane candidates, and no such pool exists (total real supply is 0.63B). At the measured 0% reasoning fill rate it cannot be filled at all from open data. Both have to be manufactured deliberately, which is exactly why the reserve is a Session-5 allocation decision and not something discovered at the end of the run.
