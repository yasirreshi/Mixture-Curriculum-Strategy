Starting position

Two constraints that shaped every method choice

Neither was chosen. Both are worth stating first, because they explain why the experiments look the way they do rather than the way a lab would run them.

No GPU. The plan commits to 1B and 3B proxy runs; this machine has CPU-only PyTorch. So the pre-registered protocol is written for the cluster, and what actually ran here is the same instrument three orders of magnitude down — 11.4M-parameter models, 3.0M tokens each. That gap is stated everywhere the results are used, and it is the reason the runs are treated as direction-finding rather than confirmation.

Hugging Face was unreachable from this network. Its API and CDN reset the connection, so no dataset could come from there. Every source was re-routed to something reachable and licence-checked: Wikimedia cirrussearch dumps, raw GitHub, Project Gutenberg, arXiv. This turned out to matter — pulling PRM800K as a raw file rather than a pre-processed dataset is what later made the L3 reconstruction possible at all.

Overturned #1 — how the agentic lane is sized

Of 11,781,899 agentic tokens, only 4,529,722 carry loss — the rest are tool schemas, user turns and observation slots the model reads but must never reproduce. The lane is 38.4% supervised. Sizing it from dataset bytes overstates its learning signal by 2.6×, so the plan now states shares in consumed tokens and tracks supervised tokens separately.

Overturned #2 — the experiment could not answer its own question

Three arms beat the plan. Two of them win by overspending scarce lanes — and this ablation samples every lane with replacement, so a bigger share always helps and no arm ever pays for repetition. Repetition is the entire basis for the agentic number, so the instrument was blind to the one thing it was aimed at. That became a required correction to the 1B protocol, and step 06.

Overturned #3 — the protected floor

Zero of 90 Indic candidate batches survive selection. A 12% absolute floor would have delivered exactly 12% against a 17.9% schedule — a 5.9-point shortfall in every batch across 2.9T tokens. The floor is no longer an absolute number: Indic 12% + agentic 0.5% + long reasoning 2% = 14.5% → 90% of each protected lane's scheduled share = 23.3% of every batch.

Overturned #4 — the 500-token prefix is not a detail

Long-context candidates retain 0.0% scored on a document's opening and 77.8% scored anywhere else in the same documents. A 78-point swing from scorer placement alone. The session says this about agentic traces; it generalises to every lane whose payload is not in the opening lines.

Confirmed — one artifact identified correctly

indic_heavy's wins over the plan on reasoning (−1.14% → +0.18%), agentic (−1.88% → +0.29%) and STEM (−0.43% → +0.33%) all flip to losses once repetition is charged for. Its step-04 lead was the instrument, exactly as diagnosed.

Overturned #5 — the agentic share, and it was the one that mattered

v5_agentic_2pct still beats the plan on the agentic lane by 5.7% while repeating an 11k-token pool 5.5 times against the plan's 2.7 — and takes the best W. The 4-epoch cap is a cliff in the arithmetic and a slope in measurement. One validity objection survives: the agentic hold-out cannot separate tool use from memorising the Gorilla/BFCL format. So 0.96% is now explicitly provisional, the 1B protocol gains an out-of-distribution hold-out to settle it, and a written rule raises the share to 2.0% together with the synthesis target, 10B → 17B, if it survives.

Overturned #6 — the burden of proof on general web

Web is the flattest of the large lanes, the cheapest to cut in loss terms. That argues against the plan's own case for holding it at 31%. Loss on web is not downstream MMLU, so it is not a refutation — but §2 now carries the objection openly and web 31% is the first number the 1B runs are pointed at. The long-context row (−0.056) measures the instrument: at context 256 the model cannot express long-context ability at all, which became the second protocol correction.

08
Build the band that does not exist 09_build_long_traces.py

Cleaning found the reachable open reasoning corpus tops out at 992 reasoning tokens — median 132, p99 778 — so bands L3 and L4 were empty. The threshold objection was obvious and had to be answered by the distribution rather than the cut-off: the mass sits an order of magnitude below the boundary, so moving the line changes the count, not the conclusion.

PRM800K is not a list of solutions; it is a tree search over them, in which humans rated 60,398 completions as wrong. Step 02 discarded all of that to get clean linear traces. Replaying the search in the order it happened — rejected attempt, correction marker, then the continuation actually chosen — is the plan's own recipe executed rather than promised.

Held — and it is the clearest argument in the plan

Lanes fed by curated sources fill trivially; a public-domain book simply is long-context reserve material. The two lanes this model exists for do not fill at all. At 0.6%, a 10B agentic reserve would need ~1.55T of cleaned agentic candidates and total real supply is 0.63B. The reserve's hard problem is manufacturing, not selection — which is why it has to be decided now rather than discovered at the end of the run.

Open

What is still unsettled

The agentic share. 0.96% survives on a validity objection, not on a win. The 1B run with an out-of-distribution hold-out settles it, and the rule for what happens either way is already written.

General web at 31%. The elasticity fit argues against it. Held for now on the grounds that held-out loss is not a downstream benchmark, but it is first in the queue.

L4 traces. Do not exist in reachable open data and cannot be reconstructed from PRM800K's search depth. Costed at 25B of distillation with a delivery gate; until that lands, the top of the effort dial is unfunded.

Everything at scale. Eleven models of 11.4M parameters cannot confirm shares for a 40B run. They were run to find direction and to catch the two protocol flaws above — which they did, at a cost of 2.2 CPU-hours against a pre-registered cluster experiment costing 0.65% of the main run.
