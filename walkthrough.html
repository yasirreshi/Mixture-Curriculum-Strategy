What the experiments established · 23 models across four runs

CONFIRMEDThe mixture is a capability decision, measurably. naive_web_heavy came last on all three instruments — on the last by 34.8× the measured noise floor. The one pre-registered kill condition never fired.

CALIBRATEDThe instrument knows its own resolution: three seeds of one mixture put the noise floor at 0.0022 on W. Four of six comparisons clear it; two do not, and are reported as unresolvable rather than as results.

OVERTURNEDTwo findings went against the plan and both are reported: it placed 4th of 6, then last of 4; and under an OOD hold-out with the plan's own loss mask, 2% agentic beat the proposed 0.96% on three seeds of three, disjoint ranges. Each produced a correction to the protocol or a funded change to supply — never a rewrite of the criterion.

OPENThe reasoning lane's 85B is a gross figure. The share needs 65.3% quality-gate survival; the one component we could measure survived at 24.5%. The largest unpriced risk here, and it is priced as a stress test rather than defended.

01

The rule that makes every later number defensible

A mixture looks like a budgeting exercise and is really a supply problem. The lanes this model exists for — agentic, reasoning, native Indic — have the least data in existence. So the plan needs a rule that makes an unfundable share impossible to write down.

The governing invariant

arXiv:2305.16264
share × 3.0T ≤ 4 × ( collected unique tokens + tokens we have costed a way to manufacture )

Applied asymmetrically, on purpose: a lane that fails loses share, and the cap is never raised. That asymmetry is the only thing separating this from a spreadsheet of round numbers — and it is enforced by a script that exits non-zero. It survived its hardest test in §6, where evidence arrived favouring a bigger share and the answer was to fund more supply rather than lift the cap.

03

The Indic slot, priced tier by tier

“…it states the split across the verified, unverified, translated and synthetic tiers rather than hiding behind a single headline number.”

Main run vs anneal — the inversion is the point

session preset: 40 / 25 / 20 / 15

40% verified does not fit. It is 208B of main-run demand against ~85B of verified supply even after the collection programme — 2.4 epochs before the anneal takes its share. So the main run takes 28% verified and the anneal runs 70%: the best Indic tokens are spent at low learning rate on a model that can use them. C + D ≤ 42% is a hard solver constraint — both are manufacturable, which is exactly why they need a ceiling.

Tokenizer fertility — why the share buys less than it sounds like

Tokens consumed per whitespace word, measured on our 32k proxy tokenizer. A Malayalam word costs 4.1× an English one — the tax the 200k production vocab exists to pay down.

The Sanskrit rule

Sanskrit gets 0% of the main run, and the rule is general: a language earns a funded pretraining slice at ≥1B unique verified tokens. Below that a share only buys repetition of a small corpus — memorisation, not competence.

It gets instead: representation in the tokenizer's balanced sample, 0.2B inside the anneal reserve where the model can generalise from little data, and a place in evaluation. Maithili, Konkani, Dogri, Santali and the rest are collection and evaluation targets now — pretraining lanes when the threshold is met.

All twelve languages plus Hinglish enter at stage A, not later: script and morphology must be learned while the embedding table is still plastic. V4's gradient incident came from raising an Indic share late against settled embeddings.

Overturned — how the lane is sized

Of 11,781,899 cleaned agentic tokens, only 4,529,722 carry loss. The lane is 38.4% supervised; the rest is tool schemas, user turns and observation slots the model reads but must never reproduce. Sizing from dataset bytes overstates the learning signal by 2.6×. Remember this — in §6 the loss mask decides an experiment.

Reasoning — a supply figure that does not survive contact

nominal 85B
Overturned — the discipline the plan was not applying to itself

Cleaning PRM800K dropped 20,056 of 26,551 records (75.5%) for containing a step a human rated wrong. For a long time the plan reported that in prose and then priced the lane against the full 85B anyway. Run properly: the 7.03% share is fundable only above 65.3% survival, and the one component where survival could be measured came back at 24.5%. Neither is adopted — PRM800K's gate is the strictest in the lane, and the other 78B is answer-verified, not step-verified. But the gap is too wide to call the share settled, so it becomes a gate: sample 2,000 traces each from OpenThoughts-3 and Nemotron before stage 1.

And the bands are funded from different places. L0–L2 draws 151.9B against the 85B collected corpus (1.79 epochs). L3+L4 draws 70.1B against the 25B distillation programme alone (2.81 epochs) — because zero collected traces reach L3. Both clear the cap; pricing only at lane level would have hidden that half this lane rests on a programme that has not run.

Long context — 7.56%

Fills it: repo-level packs (~40B, built from the code lane — the only long-context shape matching SWE-bench) · books (~25B) · arXiv, Indian court judgments and gazettes (~25B, which double as Tier-A Indic) · multi-doc needle tasks (~10B).

Packing is re-composition of tokens we already own, so packed tokens are not new unique tokens and are charged to their source lane's epoch budget. A rule the solver enforces, and one that is easy to cheat by accident — a repo pack looks like new data and is not.

05

The protected floor, and the reserve

“It fixes the protected always-on floor that the selector is not allowed to cross, and declares the anneal reserve that will be held back for the cooldown.”

What an English-and-code selector does to each lane

top-40% retention · 90 candidate batches per lane

Zero of 90 Indic candidate batches survive selection against an English-and-code proxy. The selector does not under-value Indic; it erases it — the one row that did not move when the corpus later grew 1.6×.

Overturned — the floor

A 12% absolute floor would have delivered exactly 12% against a 17.9% schedule — a 5.9-point shortfall in every batch across 2.9T tokens. So the floor cannot be an absolute number below the schedule; it must be a fraction of it. Indic 12% + agentic 0.5% + reasoning 2% = 14.5% → 90% of each protected lane's scheduled share = 23.3% of every batch. The cost is stated rather than hidden: OPUS's efficiency gain now applies to ~77% of each batch against ~92% under V4's 8% floor.

Held — the clearest argument in the plan

Lanes fed by curated sources fill trivially; a public-domain book simply is long-context reserve material. The two lanes this model exists for do not fill at all. At a 0.6% fill rate a 10B agentic reserve would need ~1.55T of cleaned candidates — and total real supply is 0.63B. Re-measured on a corpus 1.6× larger, these two did not move at all.

Quarantine is implemented, not described: admitted shards are written as physically separate files with their own content hashes, so a mis-weighted sampler cannot reach them. Reserve Indic runs 70% Tier-A against 28% in the main run; reserve reasoning runs 70% L3/L4 against 28%. The scarcest material is deliberately spent last.

Why this order

three decisions worth defending
DecisionThe evidence behind it
Indic enters at Seed, at 20% — higher than any stage until the anneal Script and morphology must be learned while the embedding table is still plastic. V4's incident is the evidence: a step change in the Hindi share met frozen embeddings and the gradient norm jumped ~150×.
Long context arrives only in stage four Batches must be length-homogeneous, and a short sample in a long batch is wasted compute. It steps 8K→16K→32K→128K once the model can already read and reason, with the RoPE-θ extension run on long-context data only.
Agentic starts at zero and ends at 10× Pretraining installs the format, not the capability — and a format lesson is wasted on a model that cannot yet read. The capability is bought in SFT and RLVR, on data reserved here.

Every stage transition is blended over an 8B-token warm-up, with a rollback trigger at gradient norm rising >3× over a 2B-token window — re-entered over 16B.

The objection this ladder has to survive

Your corpus tops out at 992 reasoning tokens and you drew the L3 line at 1024 — you engineered the result. Fair challenge, and the distribution answers it rather than the threshold does. Across 20,753 usable traces: median 132 · p99 778 · maximum 992, against an L3 boundary of 1,024.

The mass sits an order of magnitude below the boundary. Moving the line to 768 or 1536 changes the count, not the conclusion: open worked-solution data is short because MATH-style solutions are short. Reconstruction lifts the ceiling from 992 to 3,250 — real, and still short of L4's 4,096.

06·b

Where this session sits in the whole run

The lifecycle is pretraining → mid-training → SFT → reasoning training → preference alignment → serving. Session 5 owns the first two — and they are 98.8% of all training tokens.

Token budget, and the loss signal narrowing at every step

session: “pretraining is 95% of all the data”

Three things fall out of this table, and they are why it belongs in a mixture plan. (1) Nearly every training token is decided here, so a mixture error cannot be fixed downstream. (2) The loss signal narrows at every step — every token → response only → response + reward → reward alone → nothing. That is the same principle as the agentic masking convention applied within pretraining, and §7 shows it deciding an experimental result. (3) The reserve's 18B of L3/L4 reasoning is stage four's feedstock — which is exactly why trace length is banded now rather than later, and why L4 being empty is a problem to solve today rather than in Session 17.

What pretraining can and cannot buy

Pretraining installs the format — action/observation alternation, tool-call syntax, the structure of a worked multi-step argument. The capability is bought later, on data this session is responsible for reserving. That is the whole argument for a 0.96% agentic share, and the reason the reserve exists at all.

Round 2 · masked — the convention the real run uses

Mean −0.0917 nats. Three seeds of three, and the two arms' ranges do not overlap — while the 2% arm pays 5.46 epochs against 2.73.

Overturned — and the finding underneath it

The unmasked run scores the wrong tokens. The plan's own masking convention says loss never falls on tool observations or schemas — and 61.6% of agentic tokens are exactly those. An unmasked agentic score is mostly templated boilerplate the real run will never train on. Under the convention the real run uses, doubling the agentic share does improve out-of-distribution agentic loss.

Scope, because it is narrower than “2% wins”: the effect is on the agentic lane. On aggregate W the arms differ by 0.0019 against a masked noise floor of 0.0058 — not separable. The claim is precisely: doubling the agentic share reliably buys agentic capability and does not detectably cost anything else.

The loss mask is not a training detail — it decides the answer. Any ablation scoring agentic tokens unmasked is measuring tool-schema modelling and calling it tool use.

What follows — and the one thing that does not

The headline share does not move. The rule names the 1B run; this is 42M parameters. The same standard already kept code_heavy out of the table when the proxy favoured it over the plan. Accepting undersized evidence when it flatters the plan and rejecting it when it does not is the exact failure this session exists to prevent.

The supply moves now. The agentic synthesis programme is funded to 17B verified tokens immediately, not on confirmation — supply has lead time and a share does not. A share that cannot be funded when its evidence arrives is not a plan, it is a wish.

Overturned — what “starved” means depends on what you are building

The epoch-honest proxy caps each lane at its real supply scaled to the budget — which only works while the local corpus can fill the cap. General web, the lane with 4.5T of supply in the world, was held here at 3.5M tokens, capping the entire experiment at a 2.26M-token budget. The lanes with the most data on earth are the ones a local corpus holds least of relative to their supply. Collecting more lifted the ceiling to 30.2M. Collecting for the run and collecting for the experiment that validates the run are different problems — and only the first is named in the brief.

09

The ledger

What held under test

  • The 4-epoch cap — including where it now hurts. The masked result favours a share the cap makes expensive, and the response was to fund supply, not raise the cap. It has never been raised in this document.
  • The mixture beats naive web-heavy — the one pre-registered kill condition, on all three instruments; on the last by 34.8× the measured noise floor.
  • The reserve is a manufacturing problem — 0.6% and 0.0% fill on the two lanes that matter, unchanged when the corpus grew 1.6×.
  • The Indic tier deviation — 40% verified does not fit at any run size considered; 28% main / 70% anneal does.

What is still open

  • Reasoning supply — needs 65.3% survival; the one measurable component came back at 24.5%. Resolved by a sampling gate before stage 1, not by assumption. The largest open risk in the plan.
  • The agentic share — the strongest evidence here argues for 2%. The share waits for the 1B run the rule names; the supply does not wait.
  • Code-heavy — wins on every instrument, by 3.1× the noise floor on the best one. The reason web 31% is the number under most pressure.
  • L4 traces — do not exist in reachable open data. Until the 25B distillation lands, the top of the effort dial is unfunded.
